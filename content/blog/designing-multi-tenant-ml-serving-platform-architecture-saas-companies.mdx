# Designing a Multi-Tenant ML Serving Platform: Architecture for SaaS Companies

For SaaS companies, the challenge isn't just serving one model—it's serving [thousands of models](/blog/multi-model-serving-shared-infrastructure) across thousands of customers while maintaining strict isolation and [fair-share scheduling](/blog/batching-strategies-llm-inference-throughput-vs-latency). This is critical when [embedding ML features into SaaS products](/blog/ai-infrastructure-for-saas-embedding-ml-features-without-slowing-product) without impacting core application performance.

If you don't design for multi-tenancy early, a single "noisy neighbor" can destroy your [system SLOs](/blog/ai-system-slos-non-deterministic-systems) for every other customer.

## The Pillars of Multi-Tenant ML Serving

### 1. Resource Isolation
Use Kubernetes **Namespaces, ResourceQuotas, and Taints/Tolerations** to ensure that one tenant can't monopolize [GPU capacity](/blog/capacity-planning-llm-inference-gpu-memory-throughput-slas).

### 2. Fair-Share Scheduling
Implement a [gateway layer](/blog/llm-gateway-architecture-routing-rate-limits-cost-controls) that enforces per-tenant rate limits and uses weighted fair queuing (WFQ) to ensure predictable performance across the platform.

### 3. Data Privacy and Compliance
Multi-tenancy often requires meeting [GDPR and SOC 2](/blog/soc2-controls-for-ai-infrastructure-enterprise-checklist) requirements for each individual customer. This requires robust [secrets management](/blog/secrets-management-ai-pipelines-api-keys-model-weights-credentials) and [PII filtering](/blog/data-privacy-rag-systems-pii-filtering-before-model).

## Final Takeaway

Multi-tenant ML serving is an exercise in balancing isolation with efficiency. By building resource quotas and fair-share scheduling into your platform, you ensure that your AI features scale gracefully along with your customer base.
