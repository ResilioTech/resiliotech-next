# Production RAG Systems Reliability Checklist

Building a RAG prototype is easy; building a [reliable production RAG system](/blog/why-your-ml-models-fail-in-production) is hard. To move beyond a demo, you must address retrieval quality, [latency bottlenecks](/blog/why-your-llm-responses-are-slow-diagnosing-inference-latency-production), and [data privacy](/blog/data-privacy-rag-systems-pii-filtering-before-model).

## The Reliability Checklist

### 1. Retrieval Quality (The Grounding Gate)
- Are you using [automated evals (DeepEval, Ragas)](/blog/building-eval-pipeline-catches-regressions-before-users) to measure faithfulness and relevancy?
- Do you have a "Gold Standard" dataset for [A/B testing retrieval strategies](/blog/ab-testing-llm-outputs-statistical-methods-non-numeric-responses)?

### 2. Performance and Latency
- Is your [vector database](/blog/vector-database-operations-scaling-backup-disaster-recovery) right-sized for your query volume?
- Are you using [continuous batching and vLLM](/blog/serving-open-source-llms-with-vllm-kubernetes) to minimize TTFT?

### 3. Data Freshness and Lineage
- How quickly do [new features or documents](/blog/feature-store-reliability-stale-features-break-predictions) become available in the index?
- Can you [reconstruct a decision](/blog/ai-audit-logs-regulators-ask-for-how-to-prepare) based on the exact document version used?

## Final Takeaway

A reliable RAG system is a multi-layered architecture. By systematically checking each layer—from retrieval quality to [secrets management](/blog/secrets-management-ai-pipelines-api-keys-model-weights-credentials)—you ensure that your system is as trustworthy as the data it's grounded in.
