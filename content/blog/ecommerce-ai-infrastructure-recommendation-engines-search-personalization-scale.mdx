# E-Commerce AI Infrastructure: Recommendation Engines, Search, and Personalization at Scale

In e-commerce, AI is the engine behind discovery and conversion. Whether it's a [real-time recommendation engine](/blog/multi-model-serving-shared-infrastructure) or [semantic vector search](/blog/vector-database-operations-scaling-backup-disaster-recovery), your infrastructure must handle extreme traffic spikes while maintaining sub-second [latency SLOs](/blog/ai-system-slos-non-deterministic-systems).

## Scaling for Peak Traffic (Black Friday Readiness)

E-commerce traffic is bursty. Your [GPU node pools](/blog/gpu-autoscaling-right-sizing-inference-clusters) must be able to scale rapidly to handle 10x loads during sales events. We recommend using a mix of [reserved and spot instances](/blog/ai-infrastructure-cost-optimization) to balance availability with cost.

## Personalization and Feature Freshness

Effective personalization requires real-time features. Your [feature store](/blog/feature-store-reliability-stale-features-break-predictions) must be able to ingest user clickstream data and serve updated features to your models in milliseconds. If your features are stale, your recommendations will be irrelevant.

## Final Takeaway

E-commerce AI infrastructure is a game of scale and speed. By building elastic GPU pools and low-latency feature pipelines, you ensure that your discovery and personalization features can drive revenue even during the highest-traffic events of the year.
