# Building an Internal ML Platform on Kubernetes: What Actually Works

Many teams start by trying to build a generalized ML platform that solves every problem for every user. This often leads to [platform overengineering](/blog/stop-building-ml-platforms-start-shipping-ml-features) and years of development without shipping a single feature. In fact, we've found that [most companies don't need a custom ML platform](/blog/why-most-companies-dont-need-custom-ml-platform-and-what-to-do-instead). Before you build, you should carefully weigh the pros and cons of [managed platforms vs. self-hosted Kubernetes](/blog/managed-ml-platforms-vs-self-hosted-sagemaker-vertex-ai-kubernetes-compared).

The teams that succeed are the ones that start with a [single production MLOps pipeline](/blog/mlops-pipeline-kubernetes-guide) based on their current [MLOps maturity](/blog/mlops-maturity-model-where-is-your-team-and-what-should-you-build-next) and then generalize only the parts that are repeatedly painful. This requires an [SRE mindset for AI](/blog/sre-mindset-for-ai-what-platform-engineers-get-wrong-about-ml-systems), focusing on reliability and scale from the start.

## Successful Patterns for K8s ML Platforms

### 1. Abstracting the Infrastructure, Not the Workflow
Provide standardized [GPU node pools](/blog/gpu-autoscaling-right-sizing-inference-clusters) and [secrets management](/blog/secrets-management-ai-pipelines-api-keys-model-weights-credentials), but don't force every team to use the same training framework.

### 2. Standardizing the Release Path
Use **Argo CD** or **Flux** for GitOps-based deployments. This makes [canary releases](/blog/model-canary-releases-shadow-traffic-guide) and rollbacks a standard part of the platform, not a manual exception.

### 3. Integrated Observability
Embed **Prometheus and Grafana** dashboards into the platform from day one. If a user deploys a model, they should automatically get [latency and quality metrics](/blog/ai-observability-metrics-dashboards-that-matter).

## Final Takeaway

An internal ML platform is a product, not a project. By focusing on removing friction from the most common workflows first, you build a system that people actually want to use.
