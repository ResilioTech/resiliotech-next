# SOC 2 Controls for AI Infrastructure: An Enterprise Checklist

For AI startups selling to the enterprise, **SOC 2 Type II** is no longer optional. But traditional SOC 2 controls don't always map cleanly to [GPU clusters](/blog/private-ai-infrastructure-kubernetes-reference-architecture) and [non-deterministic model outputs](/blog/ai-system-slos-non-deterministic-systems).

To pass your audit, you need a compliance strategy that accounts for the unique risks of AI infrastructure.

## The AI SOC 2 Checklist

### 1. Access Control (Logical Access)
Use **OIDC and RBAC** to ensure that only authorized users can access production [model weights](/blog/secrets-management-ai-pipelines-api-keys-model-weights-credentials) or [sensitive training data](/blog/data-privacy-rag-systems-pii-filtering-before-model).

### 2. Encryption and Data Protection
Ensure all data is encrypted in transit and at rest. This includes your [vector databases](/blog/vector-database-operations-scaling-backup-disaster-recovery) and [feature store](/blog/feature-store-reliability-stale-features-break-predictions) snapshots.

### 3. Change Management and Audit Logs
Every model promotion must be documented. Use [canary releases](/blog/model-canary-releases-shadow-traffic-guide) to provide a clear record of testing and [automated evals](/blog/building-eval-pipeline-catches-regressions-before-users) before a new model goes live.

## Final Takeaway

SOC 2 for AI is about proving that your non-deterministic systems are governed by deterministic processes. By codifying your access controls, encryption, and change management today, you build the trust required to win and retain enterprise customers.
